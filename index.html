How to record a useful drive
  1. Mount the phone rigidly to the steering wheel (a clamp or strap, screen facing you). A loose mount adds its own rattle.
  2. Set tire size in Vehicle so vibrations can be matched to wheel rotation.
  3. Press Start before you drive. Keep the screen on and the app in front – Android pauses sensors in the background.
  4. Include: 30 s at standstill with the engine running, steady speed through the range where you feel it (hold each speed ≥ 20 s), a few firm stops, and a couple of strong accelerations.
  5. A passenger can press Mark when the vibration is felt. Don't touch the phone while driving.

    About the measurement. Android browsers deliver motion data at about 60 Hz, so vibrations up to ~30 Hz are measurable. That covers wheel/tire orders (≈ 5–20 Hz at road speed) and brake judder. Engine firing frequency while driving is usually higher and is not reliably captured.
